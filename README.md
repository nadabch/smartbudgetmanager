# SmartBudget Manager — README

Student: Bachir Bouiadjra Nada  |  Group: Group02  |  TP1 — Technical Documentation

## What is it?

SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook for tracking monthly income and expenses. It calculates totals and balance, compares expenses with a budget, and summarizes spending by category.

## Workbook contents

- **Dashboard:** financial totals, monthly balance, budget status and chart.
- **Transactions:** date, description, type, category, income, expense, notes and month.
- **Categories:** Food, Transport, Rent, Health and Education, with monthly budget values.

## What you need

Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365, or compatible spreadsheet software.

## Getting started

1. Open `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx` and save a working copy.
2. In Transactions, review the sample records and enter your own transactions in the first empty row.
3. Enter income in the Income column and spending in the Expense column.
4. Choose or type the appropriate expense category.
5. Review the budget values in Categories.
6. Open Dashboard to check totals, balance, budget status and the chart.
7. Save your changes.

## Important notes

Keep existing headers and formulas intact. Verify the transaction rows covered by formulas and confirm the budget warning and category selection behavior in your Excel version. The workbook is not documented as password-protected.

## Troubleshooting

If totals do not change, check that amounts are numeric, entries are within the supported rows, and formulas have not been overwritten. If results look outdated, recalculate or reopen the workbook in Excel.
